❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is ZEUS AI? ▼
ZEUS AI is an advanced AI assistant capable of answering questions, helping with coding, writing, research, translations, and everyday productivity tasks. It's designed to be your ultimate intelligent companion.
Is ZEUS AI free to use? ▼
ZEUS AI offers both free and premium tiers. Free users get access to core features with some limitations. Premium subscribers enjoy unlimited chats, priority support, and access to advanced tools.
How do I get a premium code? ▼
Premium codes can be purchased through the pricing section. You can choose weekly, monthly, or lifetime plans. Existing users can also receive codes through promotions and referrals.
Can I use ZEUS AI for coding? ▼
Absolutely! ZEUS AI excels at programming assistance across multiple languages including Python, JavaScript, C++, SQL, HTML/CSS, and more. It can help with debugging, code generation, and explanations.
Is my data secure? ▼
Yes. Your conversations are stored locally in your browser. We do not share your data with third parties. For premium users, we offer encrypted storage and enhanced privacy protections.
How do I contact support? ▼
You can reach us via WhatsApp at +234 906 676 0078, or through our social media channels on TikTok, YouTube, and WhatsApp.
